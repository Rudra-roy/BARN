How The Pipeline Works
① Straight Path: Simple pixel-to-pixel distance from robot to goal. This is the naive path — it goes through obstacles.
② B-Spline Curve: A cubic B-spline is fitted to the straight path. Gradient descent optimization pushes control points away from obstacles while maintaining smoothness (C² continuity). The curvature is bounded by κ_max = 2/W (track width).
③ TOPPRA Velocity: For every point on the B-spline, a velocity reference is computed. High curvature → slow down (inner wheel limit). Open straights → accelerate. Uses backward + forward reachability passes.
④ MPC Tracking: The robot follows the B-spline using Model Predictive Control. Each step, it rolls the motion model forward N steps to predict future positions, picks the best controls, and executes only the first step.

Sensor Comparison: ZED 2i vs RealSense D435i (15m Long-Range Scenario)

Evaluate how sensor detection range and Field of View (FOV) affect the path planning pipeline. A short sensing range forces late obstacle detection, resulting in sudden sharp curves and severe TOPPRA speed drops.

📊 Basis of Comparison Calculations:
  • Sensing Range Limit: ZED 2i stereo depth senses obstacles up to 20.0m (81° FOV), whereas RealSense D435i IR depth drops off beyond 3.5m (95° FOV). Obstacles outside 3.5m are completely invisible to RealSense.
  • Reaction & Decision Buffer: Lead time available before obstacle encounter: T_buffer = (D_detect - D_safety) / v_max. At 2.0m/s, ZED 2i provides 6.8s decision time vs RealSense's 1.2s emergency window.
  • Path Curvature (κ) & Smoothness: Early detection at 15m allows fitting a B-spline with gentle curvature (κ ≤ 0.45 m⁻¹). Late detection at 3.5m forces a tight local detour (κ ≥ 1.85 m⁻¹).
  • TOPPRA Velocity Ceiling: Speed dynamically constrained by centripetal acceleration v_max(κ) = √(a_lat_max / κ) and wheel speed ratio. High curvature forces RealSense speed down to 0.55 m/s, whereas ZED 2i maintains 1.95 m/s.
  • System Robustness Score: Composite index: 0.35 × (Range Ratio) + 0.35 × (Avg Speed Ratio) + 0.30 × (Smoothness Ratio).
